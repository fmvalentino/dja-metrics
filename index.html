Methodology

Full-text search on NASA ADS for “Dawn JWST Archive” or the standalone acronym “DJA”, published or on arXiv since 2023. Each hit is scored from its highlighted context to separate genuine archive use from a person's initials or an unrelated match, then reviewed by hand where the automatic score is ambiguous. Runnable example, reproducing the numbers above end to end: dja_discovery_example.ipynb.
